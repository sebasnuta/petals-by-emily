Ordering Is Simple

Every bouquet is made just for you. Here’s how to place your order — we typically respond in the afternoons.

1

Send us a DM

Message us on Instagram @petalsby_emily to start your order for a personalized arrangement.

2

Share the details

Tell us the date and time you need it, the bouquet size, whether you’d like natural or eternal flowers, and the occasion.

3

Secure with a deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit (by Zelle, Cash App, or exact cash) locks in your order and covers your flowers and materials.

4

Pickup or delivery

Pick up your bouquet (latest pickup is 9:00 PM) or have it delivered within about 30 miles of Easley. Pay any remaining balance at pickup or delivery by Zelle, Cash App, or cash. If you’re paying with cash, please bring the exact amount, as change isn’t available.

Our Policies

Deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required on all orders to secure your flowers and cover material costs.

Payment

Deposits can be paid by Zelle, Cash App, or exact cash. The remaining balance can be paid at pickup or delivery by Zelle, Cash App, or cash. If you’re paying with cash, please bring the exact amount, as change isn’t available.

Pickup

Please arrive at the agreed time. The latest pickup time is 9:00 PM. Respecting scheduled times keeps things smooth for everyone.

Flower care

Once your flowers leave our hands, caring for them is up to you — follow our flower care guide to keep them beautiful for as long as possible.

Delivery

Delivery is available within about 30 miles of Easley, SC. Send your address in your DM and we’ll quote the delivery fee. No shipping at this time.

Lead time & last-minute orders

Last-minute orders are welcome up to the day before, when available. For a bouquet recreated from a photo, order one to two weeks ahead so every flower can be sourced. Fresh flowers vary, so an exact match isn’t guaranteed.

Cancellations

Deposits are non-refundable. If an order is cancelled, we’re unable to take future orders, so please be sure of your date before booking.
