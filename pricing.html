How payment works

  • A 50% non-refundable deposit secures your order and covers your flowers and materials.
  • Deposit is paid by Zelle. The remaining balance can be paid in cash at pickup or delivery (please bring exact change).
  • Natural and eternal bouquets are both available — just let us know which you’d like when you order.
  • Delivery is available within about 30 miles of Easley; a delivery fee is quoted based on your address.

Add a little extra

  • Plushies can be added alongside your bouquet or nestled right on top.
  • Prefer a gift set? We can put together a custom gift basket with hand-picked goodies to go with your flowers.
  • Just mention what you have in mind in your DM and we’ll build something special.
