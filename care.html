Keeping Your Flowers Beautiful

A little care goes a long way. Follow these simple tips to help your bouquet last as long as possible.

Trim the stems

Cut stems at an angle every couple of days so they can keep drinking.

Avoid direct sun

Keep your flowers out of direct sunlight for long periods to keep them fresh.

Use the flower food

Sprinkle in the flower food we provide to keep your blooms nourished.

Change the water

Refresh the water often to keep everything clean and hydrated.

Strip lower leaves

Remove any leaves that sit below the waterline so the water stays clean and bacteria-free.

Keep away from fruit & heat

Ripening fruit and warm spots release gases that age flowers faster, so pick a cool place away from the fruit bowl.

A little extra love for lilies

  • If your arrangement includes lilies, refill the water tubes as needed to keep them fresh and blooming beautifully.
  • For all arrangements, add fresh water as needed to keep your flowers hydrated and looking their best.

Once your flowers leave our hands, caring for them is up to you — these tips help them stay beautiful as long as possible.
