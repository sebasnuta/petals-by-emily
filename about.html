Meet Petals By Emily

I’ve always been a creative, crafty person who loves giving gifts and seeing people smile. Becoming a florist brought all of that together. It lets me create something beautiful and do something kind for someone else at the same time.

Flowers have a way of brightening anyone’s day. Whether it’s for a wedding, a proposal, a birthday, or sympathy and funeral flowers when someone needs a little comfort, my goal is always the same: to make something personal that feels created just for you.

The first time I made a bouquet, I fell in love with the whole process. Flowers don’t just look beautiful, they smell amazing too, and putting them together never feels like work. I love photographing every finished arrangement, and I really light up when the seasons change. Fall, Christmas, and Valentine’s Day are perfect for holiday themed bouquets, and spooky season is my favorite of all, because that’s when my imagination gets to run wild. I’m proud to create these for friends and neighbors across Easley and the greater Greenville area.
